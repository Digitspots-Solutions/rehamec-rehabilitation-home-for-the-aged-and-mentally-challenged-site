About REHAMEC

REHAMEC stands as a beacon of hope for families across Nigeria seeking professional, compassionate care for their elderly loved ones and individuals facing mental health challenges. Located in the peaceful community of Umuada Obehie, Asa, we provide a safe, nurturing environment where dignity meets dedicated rehabilitation.

Our multidisciplinary team of healthcare professionals, therapists, and trained caregivers work together to create personalized care plans that address both physical and mental health needs. We understand the unique cultural context of Nigerian families and the importance of maintaining connections while providing specialized support.

Every resident receives individualized attention in our modern facility, designed to feel like home while offering the medical and therapeutic interventions necessary for recovery and improved quality of life.

Our Services

REHAMEC offers comprehensive rehabilitation and residential care services tailored to the needs of each individual.

Elderly Care & Rehabilitation

Specialized programs for age-related conditions including mobility support, chronic disease management, physiotherapy, occupational therapy, and memory care for dementia patients.

Mental Health Rehabilitation

Evidence-based treatment programs for individuals facing mental health challenges, including psychiatric evaluation, medication management, counseling, behavioral therapy, and social skills development.

Residential Care

Safe, comfortable accommodation with 24/7 medical supervision, nutritious meals prepared to cultural preferences, recreational activities, and spiritual support.

Family Support Services

Regular family consultations, caregiver training, transition planning, and ongoing communication to ensure families remain involved in their loved one's care journey.

Therapeutic Programs

Group therapy sessions, art and music therapy, physical rehabilitation, cognitive stimulation activities, and community integration programs designed to promote independence and wellbeing.

Our Professional Team

REHAMEC's strength lies in our dedicated team of qualified professionals who bring both expertise and genuine compassion to their work. Our staff includes licensed medical doctors, registered nurses, mental health specialists, physiotherapists, occupational therapists, nutritionists, and trained caregivers—all committed to providing the highest standard of care.

Each team member undergoes continuous professional development to stay current with best practices in geriatric and mental health care. We maintain appropriate staff-to-resident ratios to ensure personalized attention and immediate response to any needs. Our team understands the cultural sensitivities important to Nigerian families and works collaboratively with families to honor individual preferences while delivering evidence-based care.

At REHAMEC, every team member is united by a single mission: restoring dignity, independence, and hope to those we serve.

Frequently Asked Questions

What conditions does REHAMEC treat?

We provide care for elderly individuals requiring assistance with daily living, chronic disease management, dementia care, and post-surgical rehabilitation. We also offer specialized mental health rehabilitation for various conditions including depression, anxiety disorders, schizophrenia, and bipolar disorder.

What are your admission requirements?

We require a medical assessment, relevant medical records, and a consultation with our clinical team. We work with families to ensure our facility can meet the specific needs of each potential resident.

Can families visit residents?

Absolutely. We encourage family involvement and have flexible visiting hours. Family participation is an important part of the recovery and care process.

What is included in the care fees?

Our fees cover accommodation, meals, 24/7 medical supervision, prescribed therapies, recreational activities, and basic medical supplies. Specialized medications and procedures may incur additional costs, which we discuss transparently during admission.

Is REHAMEC licensed?

Yes, REHAMEC operates in full compliance with Nigerian healthcare regulations and maintains the necessary licenses and certifications for residential care and rehabilitation services.

How do I know if residential care is the right choice?

Our admissions team offers free consultations to help families assess their options. We can discuss your loved one's specific needs and whether our services align with their care requirements.
