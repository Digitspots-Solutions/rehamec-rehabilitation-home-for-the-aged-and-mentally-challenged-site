About Us

Mikcoson Leasing Limited, also operating as Mikcoson Equipment Finance Co, is a leading financial services institution specializing in equipment leasing and asset financing across Nigeria. Based in Umuada Obehie Asa, we serve businesses nationwide with tailored financial solutions that drive operational efficiency and business expansion. Under the experienced leadership of our Trust Company Director, we have built a reputation for integrity, transparency, and deep understanding of Nigerian business needs. Whether you're in construction, manufacturing, agriculture, healthcare, or technology, we structure financing packages that align with your cash flow and growth objectives. Our mission is simple: to remove financial barriers and give Nigerian enterprises the tools they need to compete and thrive in today's economy.

Our Financing Process

Simple Steps to Equipment Ownership

1

Initial Consultation

Contact us via phone or WhatsApp to discuss your equipment needs and business situation.

2

Application Submission

Complete our straightforward application form with your business registration documents, CAC papers, and financial statements.

3

Equipment Evaluation

Our team assesses the equipment value, useful life, and suitability for your business operations.

4

Credit Assessment

We review your business credit history, cash flow projections, and collateral (if required).

5

Approval & Terms

Receive your customized financing proposal with clear payment schedules and terms.

6

Documentation

Sign the lease agreement and complete all legal formalities with our guidance.

7

Equipment Delivery

We facilitate equipment procurement from approved suppliers or release funds for your purchase.

8

Ongoing Support

Throughout the lease period, we provide account management and flexible restructuring if business conditions change.
